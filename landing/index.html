❓ QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Android warn that the file might be harmful?

Android displays this standard warning whenever you download an APK directly outside of the Google Play Store (known as sideloading). Choreémon is completely open-source, contains zero trackers, no ads, and no background services. You can verify the file integrity against the SHA-256 hash provided above.

Does Choreémon require internet access?

No! The Android app is 100% offline-first. All creature data, points, and family members are saved securely in local storage on your device. It runs smoothly on airplanes, road trips, and with Wi-Fi turned off.

What is the Demo Parent PIN?

When you launch the app and tap "Start playing", you explore the preloaded Chen family demo. The demo parent PIN is 2468. You can also tap "Create our household" on the welcome screen to set your own family name and custom 4-digit PIN.

Can I have multiple kids on one device?

Yes! Choreémon supports up to 8 family members on a single phone or tablet. Each adventurer has their own wallet, creature companion, streak counter, and chore history. Switch profiles anytime from the top greeting.

What Android devices are supported?

Any Android phone or tablet running Android 8.0 (Oreo, API level 26) or newer. Built with Jetpack Compose for buttery-smooth performance.
